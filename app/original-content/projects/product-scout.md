The search bar assumes the shopper already knows the right word. Most of the time they don't — they know a problem, a vibe, an occasion. Product Scout closes that gap by matching intent to catalogue with semantic retrieval instead of keyword overlap.

It is the Indexer primitive from Poysis, deployed where the model has to earn its keep: real stores, real carts, real conversion. Product Scout is the proof that the composable-primitives thesis works in the wild before it works everywhere.
